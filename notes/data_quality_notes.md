# Data Quality Notes

## Data Understanding

The analysis uses 12 monthly Divvy trip data ZIP files in `data/raw/`, covering trips started from March 2025 through February 2026.

Raw columns used:

- `ride_id`
- `rideable_type`
- `started_at`
- `ended_at`
- `start_station_name`
- `end_station_name`
- `member_casual`

The pipeline reads the raw files in chunks because the full year contains more than 5.6 million rows.

## Cleaning Rules

The cleaning logic is implemented in `scripts/bikeshare_analysis.py` and is called from both `notebooks/02_data_cleaning.ipynb` and `scripts/clean_data.py`.

Records are removed when they meet one of these conditions:

- duplicate `ride_id` after the first occurrence
- missing `started_at` or `ended_at`
- `member_casual` is not `casual` or `member`
- `started_at` falls outside `2025-03-01 00:00:00` through `2026-02-28 23:59:59`
- ride duration is less than or equal to zero minutes
- ride duration is greater than 24 hours

Records with missing station names are preserved for time-based analysis. Blank station names are excluded only from station ranking tables.

## Created Fields

- `ride_length_min`: trip duration in minutes
- `month`: calendar month of trip start
- `day_of_week_num`: Sunday as 1 through Saturday as 7
- `day_of_week`: weekday label
- `start_hour`: hour of trip start
- `is_weekend`: Saturday or Sunday flag
- `is_commute_window`: weekday trips starting 7-9 or 16-18
- `is_round_trip`: start and end station names match and are not blank

## Known Limitations

- The data is anonymized and cannot connect rides to individual people.
- There is no demographic, income, pricing-plan history, marketing exposure, or conversion outcome data.
- Missing station fields limit station-level interpretation.
- Public trip data supports behavioral pattern analysis, not causal claims about membership conversion.
