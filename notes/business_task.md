# Business Task

## Business Context

Bike-share systems serve both annual members and casual riders. Annual members provide recurring revenue and tend to represent a more predictable customer base. Casual riders create a conversion opportunity if their observed usage patterns suggest repeat value from a membership.

## Primary Question

**How do annual members and casual riders use bike-share trips differently?**

## Target Audience

The analysis is written for marketing and business stakeholders who need a concise, evidence-based explanation of the differences between casual riders and annual members.

## Decision Supported

The analysis supports decisions about where, when, and how to focus marketing experiments aimed at converting casual riders into annual members.

## Analytical Questions

- How does ride volume differ by rider type?
- How does ride duration differ by rider type?
- How do weekday, weekend, and hourly usage patterns differ?
- How seasonal is casual rider activity?
- Which start stations are most associated with each rider type?
- Which observed patterns are relevant to conversion-oriented marketing?

## Expected Outputs

- A clear statement of the business task
- A description of data sources used
- Documentation of cleaning and manipulation decisions
- A summary of analysis results
- Supporting visualizations and key findings
- Three evidence-based recommendations
